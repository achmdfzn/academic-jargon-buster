The renormalization group is a mathematical framework for studying
systems at different scales.

In machine learning, gradient descent is used to train models, but
overfitting remains a common problem when the model memorises the
training data. Stochastic gradient descent speeds this up by using
small random samples at each step.

In economics, causal inference asks whether one thing actually causes
another, rather than merely happening at the same time. A related
complication is heteroscedasticity, where the spread of errors is
not constant across the range of values.
